Family hub installer (DevKit + knob screen)

This puts the family hub software onto the screen. You only need it once: after this the screen updates itself over WiFi.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop computer.
  2. Plug the screen into the computer with its USB cable.
  3. Click Install, choose the port that appears (it may say CP210x or CH340) and click Connect, then Install.
  4. Wait about a minute until it says it's done, then unplug it and plug it into its usual charger.
  5. The screen then shows WiFi setup: follow the steps on it with a phone.
This browser can't do it. Please use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

If no port appears, the computer may need the USB driver for the board (search for "CP210x driver" or "CH340 driver").
